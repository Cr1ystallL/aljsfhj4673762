## Design Principles Applied

### Do's ✅
- Roobert as primary typeface
- Spacious layout (4px base unit, 14px element gap)
- Midnight Canvas backgrounds, Frost White text
- 75.024px radius for all buttons
- Deep Ocean Gradient for atmospheric sections
- Subtle borders (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3))

### Don'ts ✅
- No harsh saturated accent colors
- No square or sharp borders on interactive elements
- No strong box-shadows or heavy elevation
- No tight information density
- No solid color backgrounds where gradients intended
- No arcade or cheap casino aesthetics
